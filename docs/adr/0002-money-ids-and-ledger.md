# ADR-0002: Money, IDs and the ledger

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
- Money is `BigDecimal` + ISO-4217 currency (`Money` record), stored as `NUMERIC(19,4)`. Never float/double.
  Rounding is banker's rounding (HALF_EVEN) to the currency's minor units.
- All IDs are UUIDs generated by the application (`IdGenerator`), never by the database.
- An append-only, double-entry accounts-receivable ledger records every financial effect:
  invoices DEBIT the customer; payments and credit notes CREDIT them.
  The amount a customer owes is `SUM(debits) - SUM(credits)` and is **never stored as a column**.

## Why
Same rules as PayFlow. Its Phase 7.5 rework (debit and credit landing on the same wallet) came from
modelling money flows late, so the ledger directions are fixed here, in Phase 0.
